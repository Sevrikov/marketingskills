# Ecommerce Customer Pain Research

## Workflow

1. Start with hypotheses only: what pain might this product or group solve?
2. Use deep research, internal product data, market data, known sources, and source corpus to verify or reject the pain.
3. Build a `pain_profile` before writing final content, infographic, storyboard, or video prompt.
4. Map every pain claim to proof: feature, price position, availability, comparison, source, or internal calculation.
5. Inject the verified pain into each material without fearmongering or unsupported promises.
6. QA every material against `pain_profile`.

## Required Outputs

- `pain_profile` JSON or a clear draft;
- `proof_map`: pain -> feature/spec -> evidence/source;
- `buyer_words`: phrases buyers might use;
- `objections`;
- `content_guidance` for description, article, infographic, video, Shorts, Viber;
- `do_not_claim`;
- `missing_or_risky_data`.

## Rules

- Do not invent customer reviews, statistics, emotional pain, savings, medical effects, safety outcomes, or business results.
- If the pain is plausible but unverified, mark it as a hypothesis.
- Prefer practical language: what breaks, slows down, confuses, costs more, wastes time, or creates risk.
- Link product specs to outcomes only when the link is reasonable and sourced.
- Keep the pain visible in all materials when confirmed: hook, opening, visual hierarchy, FAQ, storyboard, subtitles, and CTA.
